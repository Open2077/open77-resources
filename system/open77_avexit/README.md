# open77_avexit

The AV door animations: Rosalind Myers stepping out of her Militech Manticore in Phantom Liberty (quest q305, "border crossing", where she gets out to hand V the medal), stepping up into it, (1.2.0) the game's own boarding of a Rayfield Excalibur, from the ground into its pilot seat (side quest sq012), and (1.3.0) Corpo V's boarding of Jenkins' Excalibur in the game's opening, from the ground onto its back seat (q000), cut into five clips and shipped as the custom locomotion package `avexit`. [open77_avdoor](../open77_avdoor/) plays them when a player steps out of an aircraft or boards one; this resource only registers the package and ships its archive.

- **Starts:** on its own
- **Needs:** `requiredMods.enabled = true` (the archive is a preload: the launcher installs it before the game starts)
- **Runs on:** server and client (`register.lua` on both)

## The clips

| Clip | What | Seconds | Its door end, from the entity (right, ahead, up) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `enter` (selector 0) | standing a metre before the door: turns to it, steps up half a metre into it (her entry, 2.70 to 5.30 s) | 2.6 | its LAST frame: 0.15 m, 1.17 m, +0.53 m |
| `step` | the door: steps down about half a metre and walks out | 4.3 | its first frame: 0.64 m, -2.39 m, +0.5 m |
| `sit` (1.2.0) | standing beside the Excalibur's door: turns to it, steps up into the cabin, turns and sits down in the pilot seat (sq012, 0 to 5.60 s) | 5.6 | its LAST frame -- the root, in the seat: -2.28 m, 0.59 m, +0.96 m, turned 14.8 deg to the right |
| `step_m` | `step` mirrored, for a door on the right | 4.3 | -0.64 m, as above |
| `couch` (1.3.0, selector 4) | standing outside the Excalibur's door, facing it: steps into the folded door's tray, up onto the floor, walks in, turns left and sits down on the back-left seat facing ahead (Corpo V, q000, 0 to 4.40 s) | 4.4 | its LAST frame -- the root, on the seat: 0.45 m, 1.95 m (1.3.0: 1.55), +0.96 m, turned 90 deg to the left |

An exit clip ENDS where the player entity stands, facing forward, so a resource puts the entity at the end point and turns it so the first frame lands on the door; an enter clip STARTS there, so the entity is put that far out from the door and turned so the last frame lands in it -- one plan for both (`AvDoor.WalkOutPlan` in open77_avdoor). `sit` starts there too, and its last frame is the seat itself: the entity is put so that the clip's end lands on the seat's slot, facing the slot's way (`AvDoor.SitPlan`). Root motion is baked into the hips: the locomotion runtime plays one-shot clips without native root motion. The numbers above are the mean of the female and male cuts (they differ by 6 %), but for `sit`, whose male cut keeps the female's path across the ground so both end in the seat. 1.0.0 had `exit` and `exit_m` (from the seat, 7.3 s) in selectors 0 and 4; nothing played them (the exit starts on the door), and 1.1.0 gave their slots to the enter. 1.2.0 gave the slot of `walk` (beside the door on the ground, 2.9 s, an exit clip no setting chose) to `sit`; 1.3.0 gave the slot of `enter_m` (`enter` mirrored, 2.6 s, for a door on the right: only the Manticore has one) to `couch`, whose two cuts come from the player's own female and male animations and end on the same root.

## Using the package from another resource

```lua
-- server, a resource with permissions { "locomotion.control", "locomotion.read" }
local ok, reason = Open77.locomotion.set(playerId, "avexit", { resetOnDeath = false })
local played, handle = Open77.locomotion.playClip(playerId, "step", { speed = 1.0, blendInMs = 0, blendOutMs = 450 })
```

Only the resource that gave a player the style may play its clips on them, and the host plays none on a body in a vehicle. open77_avdoor gives the style while the body sits in the aircraft (giving a style rebuilds the body every viewer draws, so it is done out of sight), and never takes a style another resource gave.

Whether the package registered, on this side, is an export (1.1.1): `exports.open77_avexit:Status()` answers `{ ok, style, reason, side }`. On a client it is the only way to know whether a clip can be drawn at all: the server registers the package from its own files, a game only from the archive it started with, and a game started with another world's mods (`locomotion_archive_missing_restart_required`) plays nothing however the server asks. open77_avdoor reads it and boards and steps out plainly then, at once, instead of holding the body still for a step nobody sees. The failure's log line says which world the launcher prepared the game for (`Open77.session.launcherContext`): its mods' digest, against the server's own "Required mods: ... digest=" line.

## Provenance and build

Source: `ep1\animations\quest\main_quests\q305\q305_11_border_crossing\body\q305_11_border_crossing__myers.anims`, animations `myers__sit_av__2h_on_armrests__01__to__myers__stand__2h_folded__01__turn90__01` (her exit, 20.17 s) and `myers__stand__2h_folded__01__to__myers__sit_av__2h_on_armrests__01__turn270__01` (her entry, 8.57 s), `woman_base` rig, read from an installed copy of Cyberpunk 2077: Phantom Liberty with WolvenKit 8.17. The archive carries animations derived from the game's own data, for use in the game: keep that in mind before publishing it outside your servers.

`sit` (1.2.0): `base\animations\quest\side_quests\sq012\sq012_03a_av_pad\body\sq012_03a_av_pad__female_average.anims`, animation `stand__2h_phone__01__to__sit_rayfield_excalibur_lean0__2h_phone__01__turn0__av_seat__01` (7.53 s; its root motion an `animUncompressedMotionExtraction`, one key a frame). The scene `sq012_03a_av_pad.scene` plays it with its origin on the AV's `seat_front_left` slot and an offset that is the inverse of its last root key: it ends in that seat. The clip is its first 5.60 s (the root is in the seat at 5.40 s), with its step up raised 0.27 m between 1.65 and 2.70 s: the scene's AV stood lower than an Excalibur on its gear does on an Open77 server (the seat 0.69 m over the ground there, 0.96 here). Her hands keep the scene's phone pose (`2h_phone`), without the phone.

- female (`wa`): the source's own rig (`woman_base`), the joint tracks copied as they are;
- male (`ma`): world-space rotation deltas from the bind pose re-applied on `man_base`, the hips path scaled by the ratio of the two hip heights (for `sit`, the height only: its path across the ground ends in the seat);
- mirrored: reflection across the body's sagittal plane with the Left/Right joints swapped.

`couch` (1.3.0): `base\animations\quest\main_quests\part1\q113\q113_05_av_flight\body\q113_05_av_flight__player_{female,male}.anims`, animation `stand__2h_on_sides__01__to__sit_couch__AV_excalibur__01__turn270__getting_into_AV__01` (5.27 s, the player's own skeletons), which `base\quest\main_quests\prologue\q000\scenes\q000_corpo_03b_fly_to_lizzies.scene` plays with its origin on Jenkins' AV's `seat_back_left` slot and an offset that is the inverse of its last root key. That AV's couch and steps are not those of the Excalibur Open77 spawns on its gear: the clip is warped onto the latter, measured from its meshes and its door animation (the folded door's tray 0.34 m over the ground, a rim at its outer edge, the upper tread and the floor 0.62 m up, the seat's cushion 0.04 m over the origin and 0.63 m deep). Its first step is moved 0.33 m nearer the door so that it lands in the tray, each foot is raised over its own swings (over the rim, onto the tray, onto the floor) and the hips as the body comes up, the feet come out in front of the deeper seat, the hips sit 0.07 m (female) or 0.05 m (male) lower and further forward, so the back meets the seat back; each foot is put on its place by two-bone IK of its hip and knee. 1.3.1 starts it 0.40 m farther from the AV (the hips 2.35 m out from the hull's centre line, not 1.95): the player entity stands on the clip's first frame while it plays, and on Staging (2026-10-30) the AV's collision pushed a body put on 1.3.0's first spot 0.34 m out before the clip began, so the whole clip played 0.34 m off the AV -- its first step in the air outside the tray's rim instead of in the tray, the body sitting down 0.34 m left of the seat's slot until open77_avdoor's hand-over moved it in. The first step is that much longer (the right foot over its first swing, the hips as the weight goes onto it, the left foot over its swing, each back on 1.3.0's path before it lands); from the floor on the clip is 1.3.0's. The female cut is the female animation on `woman_base`, the male cut the male animation on `man_base` (the player rigs' first 71 joints, bones pointing the same way, a T-pose at rest where `woman_base` hangs its arms): no retargeting between them. `scripts/avexit/couch.py` has every number.

The four variant entities and graphs (male and female, native and mirror) are those of the Aetherfall example package with the five clip names and the bank paths changed; the graphs differ from Aetherfall's only in those names (checked at the CR2W level). The recipe, from the source animations to this folder, is in [`scripts/avexit`](../../../scripts/avexit/README.md); `package.json` records every hash (revision `3ff5278f87ee…`, the entities, the graphs, the archive and the zip; 1.3.1 changed the two `couch` banks only, the other eight are byte for byte 1.3.0's, and the graphs are unchanged).

Rebuilding changes the revision, the depot paths under `open77\locomotion\avexit\<rev>\`, the archive's name and therefore `preload_mod`: a client must restart to mount the new archive.
