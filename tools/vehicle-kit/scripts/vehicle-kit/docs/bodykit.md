# Light RP body kit

This kit is route A of the vehicle customization campaign. Its parts are visual meshes
attached to a car slot. They are built by the same pipeline as the rims (see
`../README.md`). Every mesh comes from a script: no file from the game, Nexus or any
third party is in the repository.

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `specs/lightrp-bodykit.json` | Pack spec. Resource `lightrp_vk_body`, archive `LightRPVehicleKitBody`, 30 parts, 82 models. |
| `specs/lightrp-bodykit-fit.json` | Fit table for every vanilla player car body of 2.50: 69 bodies on 71 records (the 13 Light RP dealer cars, the staff-only Rayfield Caliburn, and 55 more), generated by `body_fit.py`. Every offset in it is an **offline estimate**. |
| `specs/lightrp-bodykit-cars.json` | Design rows (rear deck or roof edge) and style refusals of the 55 bodies beyond the first 14, read off their centre-line profiles. `body_fit.py` loads it. |
| `specs/lightrp-bodykit-hides.json` | The factory parts each group hides, per body, read from every appearance of the entity by `inventory/body_hides.py`. |
| `inventory/player_body_components.py` | Component inventory of every vanilla player car record, one model per body. Output goes outside the repository. |
| `blender/make_spoiler.py` + `body_wings.py` | Ten rear wing and spoiler styles. |
| `blender/make_louver.py` | Rear-window louvers. |
| `blender/make_lip.py` | Three front lips. |
| `blender/make_canard.py` | Two canard sets. |
| `blender/make_skirt.py` | Three side skirts. |
| `blender/make_diffuser.py` | Two rear diffusers. |
| `blender/make_hood.py` | Hood scoop and hood vents. |
| `blender/make_roofrack.py` | Five roof items: basket, crossbars, tray, light bar, scoop. |
| `blender/make_bullbar.py` | Push bar and tow strap. |
| `blender/body_geom.py` | Shared geometry helpers (tubes, prisms, fillets, mount records) and the generator main. |
| `blender/body_measure.py` | Measures the stock bodies. Output goes outside the repository. |
| `blender/body_surface.py` | Exact 1 cm body surfaces and per-component bounds, cached outside the repository. |
| `blender/body_fit.py` | Turns those surfaces into the fit table. |
| `blender/body_board.py` | Owner review board (Workbench). |

## Build

```powershell
pwsh -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/vehicle-kit/build-pack.ps1 -Spec scripts/vehicle-kit/specs/lightrp-bodykit.json
```

The build prints `VK_PACK_OK revision=<12 hex> models=82 files=164 ...`. It never touches the game
folder. Builds of this kit so far:

| Revision | Models | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `c774256c18e1` | 16 | First kit: four wings, lip, skirts, rack, bar. |
| `ed9c4dea033d` | 82 | This kit: 30 parts. |

The revision hashes the spec and every `blender/*.py` and `native/*.cjs` file. Any edit
to those files changes it, including edits to `body_fit.py` and `body_board.py`. The fit
table is not part of it.

Regenerate the fit table and the board after the build. Both read the build's
`work/parts` folder:

```powershell
python scripts/vehicle-kit/blender/body_measure.py --extract   # once; needs OP77_GAME_DIR
python scripts/vehicle-kit/blender/body_fit.py                 # builds the surface cache on first use
blender --background --factory-startup --python scripts/vehicle-kit/blender/body_board.py -- `
    --spec scripts/vehicle-kit/specs/lightrp-bodykit.json --fit scripts/vehicle-kit/specs/lightrp-bodykit-fit.json `
    --parts artifacts/vehicle-kit/lightrp_vk_body/work/parts --out artifacts/vehicle-kit/lightrp_vk_body/board
```

`body_fit.py --ids a,b --cars x,y --out <file>` fits a subset into another file, for
debugging. `body_board.py --only parts|cars|overview --cars ... --ids ...` renders a
subset.

For every vanilla player car (the table as committed), measure from the player inventory
instead of the dealer one (see "Which cars" below); the fit then reads
`specs/lightrp-bodykit-cars.json` and `specs/lightrp-bodykit-hides.json` by default:

```powershell
$inv = "$env:TEMP\op77-vehicle-inventory\vehicle-body-components-player-2.50.json"   # inventory/player_body_components.py
python scripts/vehicle-kit/blender/body_measure.py --extract --dealer $inv --cars all
python scripts/vehicle-kit/blender/body_fit.py --dealer $inv
```

`body_measure.py` takes about a minute per car and `body_fit.py` about 35 minutes for the
69 bodies (the 1 cm surfaces are cached beside `measure.json`). Their work folder holds
about 1.3 GB of extracted meshes for the 69 bodies.

## Parts and finishes

All dimensions are in metres. Each part has one LOD. Model ids are `<id>_<finish>`, for
example `asset:lightrp_vk_body/wing_gt_carbon`.

The materials are plain colours from the spec palette, with no texture. `carbon` is a

|---|---|---|
| `carbon` | carbon | gloss black |
| `white` | white | gloss black |
| `matte` | matte black | gunmetal or gloss black |
| `black` | gloss black | gloss black, dark metal or matte black |
| `red` | gloss red | gloss black |
| `yellow` | signal yellow | gloss black |

Three finish names are new in this kit: `red`, `yellow` and `matte`. `diffuser_race_red`

## Fit method

### Measuring the stock bodies

`body_measure.py --extract` (plain Python) uses WolvenKit CLI in read-only mode. For each
car it extracts the `.ent`, the `.app` (player appearance), the deformation rig and the
exterior meshes, into `%TEMP%/open77-vehicle-kit-measure/bodykit`. The script refuses a
work folder inside the repository, and nothing it extracts is committed.

**Which cars.** The first 14 are the Light RP dealer cars and the Caliburn
(`docs/generated/vehicle-components-dealer-2.50.json`). Since 2026-11-07 the kit covers
every vanilla player car: `inventory/player_body_components.py` reads every
`Vehicle.*_player*` record of the installed game (88 on 2.50) and keeps 71 car records on
69 bodies. Records that share an entity and the same exterior components (name and mesh
of the player appearance) share one key and one measurement: `caliburn` carries
`v_sport1_rayfield_caliburn_02_player`, `shion` carries `v_sport2_mizutani_shion_base_player`.
Left out:

- bikes (15 records): no body panel for these parts;
- AVs and panzers: none of them is a `_player` car record on 2.50, and the scripts skip
  them by type anyway;
- **quest variant records** `Vehicle.q<digits>_*` (`q000_hym_player_car`, a Hella;
  `q304_songbird_escape_player`, a Cortes): owner decision of 2026-11-07. They are neither
  measured nor listed as siblings of the Hella and the Cortes, so no fit reaches them.

Pass `--dealer <player inventory>` and `--cars all` to `body_measure.py`,
`body_surface.py`, `body_fit.py` and `body_board.py` to work on all 69 bodies. The 14
first bodies measure byte-identical either way.

It then assembles the exterior panels in the vehicle frame. The assembly composes three
things:

- the rig bind pose;
- the entity slot table;
- the appearance's local transforms.

Bikes (kusanagi, apollo) are out of scope.

Validation: the assembled bounds match each car's appearance proxy mesh (a whole-car
silhouette) within a few centimetres. Two filters keep the numbers clean:

- Factory accessories are measured on their own, so the body numbers ignore them:
  `roof_rack`, `v_common_suitcase_01`, `junk_solar`, `wing_a`, `wing_b`, `wing_c`,
  `trunk_spoiler`. `wing_b` and `wing_c` are the two blades of the Caliburn's active rear
  wing (`wing_a_01` is its centre); no other car has a component by those names. On the
  55 other bodies, every name of the car's hide list (`specs/lightrp-bodykit-hides.json`)
  is an accessory too, so a factory part a kit part replaces never counts as body.
- Panels more than 8 cm outside the proxy are dropped. This removes the bandit's rooftop
  fans, which the simple assembly misplaces by 2 m.

`measure.json` holds the numbers: bounds, wheel centres, ground height, roof run and
width, front and rear bumper bottom and width, sill line, slot bind poses.

`body_surface.py` rasterizes the same panels into **1 cm surfaces**:

- `top` and `bottom`: the highest and lowest z over (x, y);
- `right` and `left`: the largest and smallest x over (y, z);
- `front` and `rear`: the largest and smallest y over (x, z).

It also records each component's bounds. These name the rear glass, the hood, the
exhaust tips (`rearSides`) and the lids. The cache lives beside `measure.json`, outside
the repository. It is keyed by the measurements, so a re-measure rebuilds it.
`body_measure.py` still writes 4 cm height maps (`maps.json`), but the fit no longer uses
them. They keep the highest sample per cell, which hides 1 to 3 cm on a sloped deck:
enough to say where a part goes, not whether it touches.

### Frames

All frames use metres, X right, Y forward, Z up.

- **Root** is the entity frame (`props.attach` bone `''`). Wheel centres sit at z = 0.
- **Base** is the `vehicle_slots` bone the body shell is bound to. On all 69 bodies its
  bind pose is a pure translation of (0, 0, 0.27..1.00) from Root (0.27 on the MaiMai,
  1.00 on the Mackinaw BMF monster truck). So `Base offset = Root offset - base`.
- **Lid slots** (`trunk_a`, `trunk_b`, `hood_a`) are also pure translations.

`fit.offset` is given in Base. Base is the primary slot because the body shell itself is
bound to it, so a part on Base should follow body roll the way the panels do. Each entry
also carries `evidence.root.offset` for bone `''`, the binding E0.9 proved and the one
`/vkit.wing` + `/vkit.move` use.

A part is posed as **scale (per axis), then pitch about +X, then offset**. Pitch is
right-handed: a positive pitch turns +Y toward +Z. So a part that extends toward -Y (a
louver, a roof spoiler) slopes down with a positive pitch, and the top of a push bar
leans back.

When every contact point of a part lies on a lid panel, `evidence.lid` gives the offset
in that lid's slot frame, so the part opens with the lid. On semimaru the slot names
swap: its rear engine cover is the component `hood_a`, bound to the slot `trunk_a`.
On the Caliburn only the mesh names swap: the component `hood_a` (front trunk lid, mesh
`..._trunk_01`) is on slot `hood_a`, and the component `trunk_a` (rear engine cover, mesh
`..._hood_01`, with the engine glass `window_b`) is on slot `trunk_a`.

### Mount records

Every generator writes three things into the `mount` record of its `part.json`:

- `contact`: points of the part that must touch the body, with the direction they rest
  in. `down` rests on a top surface, `up` hangs under a bottom surface, `out` presses
  against a side, `fwd` and `back` press against the front or rear face.
- `embed`: boxes that are allowed inside the body. Examples are wing feet, a bonded base,
  a canard's root tongue and a push bar's mounting arms.
- `anchors`: points that must be inside the body, such as the top of a splitter rod.

**Rest.** The contact solver moves the part along the contact direction. If the gaps
under the contact points vary by `sink` or less, no point floats and the deepest one
enters the body by that spread. If they vary more, the nearest point enters by `sink`
and the rest floats. The leftover is reported as `float`.

**Audit.** The audit then samples every triangle of the placed part every 3.5 cm and
checks each sample against the body's visual hull. A sample is inside the hull only when
it is inside the top/bottom column, the side band and the front/rear band at once.
Samples in embed boxes are skipped. A sample more than 6 mm inside is a cut; a visible
cut deeper than 12 mm refuses the pair. A placer can mark samples that cannot be seen
(under the car, behind a bumper); their cuts are reported as `hiddenCut`, not refused.
The audit also checks:

- the tyres: a cylinder at each measured wheel centre, plus a 2.5 cm margin;
- the ground at rest: 5 cm for most parts;
- the anchors.

Every placer searches its free parameters (where along the deck, how wide, which pitch)
for the best audit, and refuses the pair with a reason when nothing passes.

### Placement rules (`body_fit.py`)

Each per-car design choice sits in the `DESIGN` table at the top of the script: deck
range, roof edge, and two roof overrides. Pairs refused on style live in
`STYLE_EXCLUDE`.

**Pedestal wings** (street, gt, rally, low, dual, drift, bat):

- The feet stand on the rear deck, between its rear edge and its front limit.
- The script searches along the deck for the position nearest to "trailing edge flush
  with the deck's rear edge" where the feet sit flat and nothing cuts the body.
- On a sloped deck the stands enter the surface; this is reported as `sink`, with a
  maximum of 8 cm.
- The x scale keeps the tips 6 cm inside the body, within 0.75 to 1.1.
- Hatches and vans have no deck. On the hatches, only the rally wing stands on the roof's
  rear edge, with z scale 0.55 (shorter stands).

**Duck and trunk lip:**

- The trailing edge sits on the deck's (or roof's) rear edge, or up to 25 cm ahead of it
  where the flat-bottomed part can sit.
- It is pitched to the surface (up to 16 degrees).
- It is narrowed until it rests within 6 mm. It is refused below 0.9 m, or when more than
  1.2 cm would float.

**Roof spoiler:**

- The bonded base sits on the roof's last centimetres. The roof edge is the design value
  or the first 20-degree fall of the centre line, refined to any sharp step within 15 cm.
- It is as wide as the roof stays level (within 2 cm), from 0.75 to 1.3 times its span.
- It is pitched to the roof, then lifted at the rear in 2-degree steps (up to 10) until
  the visor clears the glass.
- It is refused when more than 1 cm would float at the base's corners.

**Louvers:**

- The rear glass is the `window_b` component (or its trim variants).
- The glass must slope between 8 and 50 degrees. On steeper or vertical glass, louvers
  read as a cage.
- The panel is scaled to the glass, pitched to it (5 to 55 degrees) and rested on its
  pads. On curved glass it is trimmed in width and length until the pads rest.
- It is refused when more than 1.5 cm would float.

**Lips:**

- The pivot sits on the bumper's lower edge. The script searches from the nose back over
  45 cm, at most 10 cm behind the face.
- At each row the blade is as wide as the bumper allows, from 4 cm inside the lower body
  width down to 0.9 m. Its front strip must rest within 2 cm.
- It is pitched up to 8 degrees to the chin.
- Cuts more than 8 cm behind the edge are under the car: they are noted, not refused.
- The race lip's rod tops are anchors: they must end inside the bumper face.

**Canards:**

- The roots sit on the bumper's side faces, between the front wheel arch and the nose.
  The script tries six heights and every 1 cm along the side.
- The x scale puts the root tongue's end 8 mm inside the nearest face.
- The search prefers roots where the root is buried least and the tips stay within the
  body's width, front wing included.
- It is refused when the bumper corner curves so much along the root that half the
  plane would be buried.
- It is refused when the tips would stand more than 5 cm outboard of the widest body
  panel at their height.

**Skirts:**

- The outer face sits 1.5 cm proud of the **median** sill line between the arches.
- y is scaled to the arch-to-arch run.
- Where the sill bulges locally, the skirt steps out to the 70th, 85th or 95th
  percentile. It is refused when that leaves it standing more than 5 cm off the rest of
  the sill.
- It is refused by style where the factory body already has something at the sill line:
  hella's player-trim kit (`body_player_01`) carries flared rocker panels from arch to
  arch, and maimai has a side-step tube under its doors. A skirt there would hang under
  them as a loose bar (a shortened hella skirt was tried and looked like one).
- z is squashed to keep 7 cm to the ground. The skirts are refused below 45 %.

**Diffusers:**

- The plate's rear edge sits at the rear bumper's lower edge, at most 10 cm under the
  face. Further in, the plate would sit out of sight under the bumper (maimai's lowest
  level band is 11.5 cm in).
- It is narrowed to the level band of the bumper bottom, to 5 cm inside the exhaust tips
  and clear of the rear tyres. It is refused below 0.5 m.
- It is pitched to follow the bumper bottom along the centre line (-20 to 15 degrees).
- It is refused where the bumper bottom comes down more than 5 cm below the plate within
  15 cm of its exit: the plate would stick out as a ledge.
- It needs 7 cm to the ground.

**Hood scoop and vents:**

- They sit on the `hood*` component, or on the front deck ahead of the windscreen when
  there is no hood. The hood must be at least 0.55 m long.
- They are placed at 50 % (scoop) or 58 % (vents) along it, or at the nearest position
  that rests. They are pitched to the hood.
- The vents are spread to the hood width (0.8 to 1.15).
- They are refused when the hood slopes more than 24 degrees, or when more than 1.5 cm
  would float.

**Roof items:**

- The basket, tray and bars are centred on the roof run (the part of the centre line
  within 4 cm of the top).
  - The basket and tray are 85 % of the run long; the bars span 62 %.
  - Their feet stand 7 cm inside the roof's half-width, with an x scale of 0.75 to 1.3.
- The light bar sits at the front of the run, 6 to 14 cm behind it. It is pitched at
  most 6 degrees, since it is aimed level.
- The scoop sits 30 to 36 cm behind the front of the run.
- Each item is pitched to the slope and rested.
- An item is refused when the frame would cut the roof, or when more than 1 cm would
  float.

**Push bar:**

- The bottom sits on the bumper bottom, keeping at least 10 cm to the ground.
- The top is 2 cm under the hood's leading edge, with a z scale of 0.55 to 1.2.
- The legs sit at 55 % of the bumper half-width.
- The hoop stands **2 cm ahead of the front face** wherever it covers it. It may lean
  back by up to 12 degrees to follow a raked nose.
- The four mounting arms (48 cm long, inboard at about ±0.3 m) then bridge the gap and
  run into the bumper. Each arm must meet the face and enter it by at least 6 cm.
- It is refused when the arms would end in the air, or when the nearest arm on a side
  bridges more than 24 cm.
- It is refused when the nose rakes back more than 25 cm over the bar's height.

The first kit rested the arms on the face instead. The face is rarely flat across the
bar, so the hoop either floated 12 to 36 cm in front of the nose or cut into it.

**Tow strap:**

- The anchor plate sits on the bumper face, 24 to 46 cm left of the centre line and 4 to
  26 cm above the bumper bottom.
- It is refused when more than 6 mm of the plate would float.

### Fit table format

Every fit entry carries:

- `estimate = "offline"`;
- an `evidence` record:
  - the measured numbers it used;
  - `contact` (float and sink) and `audit` (cut, ground, tyre and hidden cut);
  - the root offset, and the lid offset when there is one;
  - `notes`: sink, tight clearances, narrowing, low ground clearance.

`groups` lists the parts of each group, with the label above.

To build `Open77.vehicles.registerParts`, keep `kind`, `slot`, `label`, `variants` and,
per record, `offset`, `rotation`, `scale` and `hide`. Drop `excluded`, `evidence` and
`estimate`.

so other trims' names are listed too.

| Group | Car | Hidden components |

  - Then each part gets a tile pair: a 3/4 view framed on it, and an orthographic

- `overview.png` (`--only overview`): the bodies alone, four views each (69 rows with

## Known limits

- **Offline only.** No offset has been seen in game.
- **Scale.** Most parts depend on per-axis `scale`: skirts, lips, louvers, diffusers,
  roof items, push bars. An unscaled skirt has its outer face at x = 1.0 m, so on a car
  with a sill at 0.8 m it floats 20 cm off the body. Whether attached props honour
  `scale` (`visualScale`) is the first thing to check.
- **Rotation.** `rotation` is a pitch about the slot X axis. Its sign in the engine still
  has to be checked (see "Frames").
- **Rigid shapes on curved bodies.** Sink and float of 1 to 2 cm are expected and listed
  in `evidence.notes`. Wing stands may enter a sloped deck by up to 8 cm (bandit,
  hozuki).
- **Hidden geometry.** Push-bar arms run up to 44 cm inside the bumper, and some lip and
  diffuser plates run into the underbody out of sight. In game they are hidden only if
  the bumper meshes are closed there.
- **Flat colours.** No texture, so carbon has no weave (see the README's material limit).
- **Mud flaps were dropped.** The bodies sit so low and their arches run so far into the
  bumpers that a flap fitted only hella and thrax, at 40 % of its height.
- **Three meshes the assembly cannot load.** The player appearances of legatus
  (`windshield_bullet_holes`, on `window_f`), outlaw_heist (`body_lights_a`, on `Base`)
  and shion_nomad (`stwheel_01_shadow`) name a mesh whose depot hash is in no archive of
  the 2.50 install (base and EP1 listings). They are left out of the measurement. The
  engine is expected to fail to load them too (so they would not render); if one does
  render in game, it is a glass, light or shadow mesh, not a body panel the parts rest on.
- **The Delamain's trunk lid is not placed offline.** On cortes_delamain the component
  of `v_standard2_villefort_cortes__ext01_trunk_02` binds to `deformation_rig` without a
  slot name, which the assembly cannot pose. The rear deck is therefore unmeasured: the
  wings and louvers are refused there with that reason, until the lid is seen in game.
- **Factory devices.** Turrets, missile launchers, roof cages and roof devices of the
  nomad, Kurtz and military builds are part of the measured surface. Where they crown the
  roof or the deck, the roof items or the wings are refused by style with the device
  named, rather than measured against it.
